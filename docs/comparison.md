# Comparison with existing products (verified 2026-10-24)

Sources are vendor or third-party pages retrieved on 2026-10-24; vendor claims are self-reported.

| Product | What it does well (per source) | Gap for a small vet-oncology startup | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clarivate **Cortellis Competitive Intelligence** | Curated pipeline data (100K+ drugs, 283K+ companies), alerts, ML-based launch timing and success-rate predictions, APIs | Enterprise subscription; pipeline intelligence is not connected to the user's own milestone budgets and cash runway | https://clarivate.com/products/biopharma/portfolio-strategy-business-development/competitive-intelligence-and-analytics/ |
| **Evaluate Pharma** (Evaluate) | Consensus sales forecasts for ~6,000 drugs linked to pipelines; AI analyst "Ask Ella" | Market and forecast data for human pharma; no internal budgeting, no veterinary focus stated | https://www.evaluate.com/product/evaluate-pharma |
| **AlphaSense** | Generative search over 10,000+ content sources (filings, broker research, transcripts); pharma CI use cases | General market intelligence; not tied to milestone funding or regulatory mapping by product type and species | https://alpha-sense.com/solutions/pharma-and-biotech-competitive-intelligence |
| **Pigment / Planful** (FP&A) | Scenario planning, budgeting, forecasting connected to accounting systems | Finance tools without scientific milestones, regulatory or competitor context; described as heavy for early startups | https://www.cbinsights.com/company/pigment-1 · https://runway.com/blog/best-modern-fpa-software-runway-vs-pigment-vs-anaplan-vs-adaptive |
| Spreadsheets + manual web search | Flexible, free | Manual reconciliation, no provenance, easy to double count or mix currencies, no change tracking | — |

**What is specifically new here** is not "using AI". It is the link between (1) milestone-level budgets and a deterministic, currency-aware cash forecast, (2) a funding test for the next scientific milestone, and (3) external evidence (competitor events, official guidance by jurisdiction and veterinary/human product type) that is cited, dated, and mapped to the same milestones for review, at a cost of cents per day. The large platforms above have far deeper curated data; this app is a lightweight operating layer for a team that cannot justify enterprise subscriptions, and could later ingest such data where licensed.
