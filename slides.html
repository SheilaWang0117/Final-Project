01 · Problem

Small vet-oncology teams make funding decisions on cash they can't see clearly.

Finance without science

A spreadsheet runway (cash ÷ burn) ignores the studies about to start, so it overstates how long the money lasts.

Science without finance

A slipped study or a new regulatory expectation is discussed in a meeting, but never turned into a dated cash effect.

Evidence without provenance

Competitor news and guidance are forwarded without date, status (draft or final) or applicability, across 5 jurisdictions and 4 languages.

Built around the three questions a manager asks every week.

Deterministic runway, per milestone

Actual spend to date vs estimate to complete; cash, burn and forecast from tested code, never from the AI.

What changed outside?

Evidence by pipeline

Competitors, products and latest movements; FDA CVM and EMA guidance quoted verbatim.

Anything else?

Ask in plain words

12 read-only tools; every figure grounding-checked; cost shown per answer.
